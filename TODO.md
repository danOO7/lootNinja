# Concept: GiftScout – Social-Aware Web Agent Gift Finder

## One-liner:
“Tell it age, gender, and budget; GiftScout scans what people are actually talking about online, then surfaces live products with images, prices, and buy-links.”

Unlike existing AI gift finders that mostly prompt over static catalogs or preloaded product feeds, you’ll emphasize:
	•	Live social chatter (Reddit, TikTok/YouTube summaries, blogs, gift guides).  
	•	Live product availability & pricing on real stores via Tavily search.  
	•	An agent loop that: understands the persona → researches trends → finds concrete items → filters by budget → outputs a shoppable list.

That’s exactly the “web as an action space” story judges want.

### Requirements:
1. Describe your person for the gift
1. Max budget
1. Find on tiktok
